## Interpretation boundaries

These are descriptive reanalyses of existing runs, not independent validation. The stability factor changes comfort accounting but not movement timing. Noise perturbs the synthetic stability signal only. Configurations differ in multiple rules, so comparisons are not clean causal ablations. Completion fractions and matched scenario uncertainty should accompany wait comparisons. See paper/manuscript.pdf and results/reproduction_check.json.
