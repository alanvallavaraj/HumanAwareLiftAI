# Publication artifacts

The revised manuscript, declarations and experiment history are in ../paper/. The main text reports the corrected 16,848-run matched-arrival campaign as primary evidence and uses the historical campaign to show how completed-trip means behave. The superseded legacy event-recording campaign and the historical replay-parity result are in Appendix B. The LaTeX source is canonical; the current PDF has 19 pages, eight main tables, four figures and three appendix tables.

Manuscript figures: conditional_contrasts.pdf is generated by scripts/make_figures.py; aei_ablation.pdf, aei_dwell.pdf and aei_weights.pdf are generated by scripts/make_aei_extension_figures.py. restricted_waits.pdf and completion_by_group.pdf belong to the superseded legacy campaign and are not part of the submission. `scripts/build_submission_package.py` writes submission-source.zip with each figure as a separate file. Historical planning and validation records are supplementary provenance, not current scientific conclusions.

All datasets are in this repository and in the Zenodo archive cited by the manuscript. The completed corrected campaign and all 22,998,183 passenger records are documented in extension_report.md. Raw event records are stored in checksum-verifiable repository parts; ../REPRODUCING.md gives restoration, analysis and replay commands.
